# Cookie Policy

This page summarizes the cookie-related practices described in the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md), effective 6 September 2026.

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by your browser. This page describes the cookie-related practices stated by the Yakka Works website and its linked services.

## Cookies used by the website

The website may use an anonymous session cookie for analytics. It is intended to last for the current browsing session and is not intended to identify a person personally. The website does not currently require a traditional account or login system.

Third-party services linked from this website, including advertising platforms, may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Yakka Works does not control the cookie practices of those services.

## Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Changes to cookie settings may affect the behaviour of third-party services embedded in or linked from this site. For Google advertising preferences, visit [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

For more information about information that may be collected, see the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
