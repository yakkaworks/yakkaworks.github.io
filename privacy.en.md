Please read the full document below for the complete terms and details.

## Accounts

Yakka Works does not currently require user accounts or logins. The website is primarily anonymous.

## Information collected

Yakka Works may collect limited anonymous analytics information, including pages visited, download interactions through Linkvertise, approximate country or location information, search queries, and anonymous session information. The purpose is primarily to understand website usage, content interest, and general metrics. No personal profile is intentionally created from this information.

## Cookies

The website may use an anonymous session cookie for analytics. The cookie is intended to last for the current session and is not intended to identify a person personally. The website does not currently require a traditional account or login system.

## Search queries

Search queries may be collected anonymously as part of website analytics, to help us understand what visitors are looking for and improve the website. Search data is not presented as personally identifying information.

## Third parties

Yakka Works links to and relies on third-party services, including Google/Google AdSense, Linkvertise, GitHub, Patreon, Saweria, TheoTown, Kustom, Facebook, YouTube, X, Instagram, and other external services. These services may have their own data collection, cookies, analytics, privacy policies, and terms. Yakka Works does not control how third-party platforms process information, and you should review their own privacy policies for details on how they handle your data.

## Infrastructure

GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, and Cloudflare Workers may be used to host or provide parts of the website. Technical information may therefore be processed by these infrastructure providers as necessary to deliver website functionality. Yakka Works does not independently control every technical request log generated by these providers.

## Email

Visitors can contact Yakka Works through [yakkaworks@gmail.com](mailto:yakkaworks@gmail.com). Email communications may contain information voluntarily provided by the sender, and email is handled through Google's email infrastructure. For privacy-related requests, please use a clear subject line such as "Privacy Request."

## Minors

The website itself does not require a user account. However, third-party services used alongside Yakka Works may have their own age restrictions and policies, and users must comply with those policies. Certain commissions or content may also have age restrictions, and NSFW commission requests must not be placed by minors. Yakka Works may refuse requests where age or platform restrictions make the request inappropriate or unavailable.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website and services evolve.

## Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to [yakkaworks@gmail.com](mailto:yakkaworks@gmail.com).
