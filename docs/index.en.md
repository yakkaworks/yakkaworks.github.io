## About Yakka Works

Yakka Works is an independent studio creating plugins and resources for the TheoTown community since 2020. Alongside TheoTown plugins, it also publishes selected Kustom presets for Android and other digital content through this website. The focus is on making accessible, useful resources for communities.

## Why Yakka Works?

- Developing TheoTown plugins since 2020, with more than **1.75 million total downloads**.
- Official staff member of the TheoTown Discord server.
- Holds the **Trusted Creator** role in the TheoTown community.
- Featured as **Best Creator of the Month** in June 2023.
