## Plugin Store Rejection Troubleshooting

**Initial diagnosis is FREE. Fixing work starts from $4.99.**

If a plugin is rejected by the TheoTown Plugin Store, Yakka Works can help review the likely reasons for rejection and identify the fixes that may be needed.

- Initial inspection and diagnosis are free of charge.
- Actual fixing work after diagnosis is a paid service.
- $4.99 is the starting price for fixing work, not a fixed price for every case.
- The final price depends on the cause of rejection, complexity, and amount of work required.
- Approval or acceptance by the TheoTown Plugin Store is not guaranteed.

Ordering and fixing work follow the terms for Full Custom Plugin Development on this page, including request, payment, revision, and licensing policies.

## Terms and Conditions

### Request policy

**Requests we may consider:**

- NSFW, with some exceptions.
- Furry.
- Mecha.
- Other custom concepts, provided they are technically feasible.

**Requests we do not accept:**

- Skyscrapers, stadiums, or similar buildings.
- Overpowered (OP) plugin features.
- Excessive in-game income.
- Excessive effect radius.
- Excessive capacity.

Some NSFW content or kinks are not accepted. Every request remains subject to technical feasibility, available capabilities, and the creator's decision. Yakka Works reserves the right to decline requests that do not meet this policy or cannot reasonably be completed.

### Revisions

- A maximum of **3 revision requests** is allowed after the initial delivery.
- Revisions are strictly limited to adjustments within the project scope agreed upon at the beginning.
- New features, additional work, concept changes, or requests outside the original scope are not revisions.
- Work outside the original scope may require additional payment and must be agreed upon in advance.

### Ordering and payment

- Orders below the applicable deposit threshold do not require an upfront deposit.
- Orders at or above the applicable deposit threshold require a **25% deposit**.
- For orders that do not require a deposit, payment is due after the work is completed.
- Available payment methods: PayPal, Patreon, and Ko-fi. QRIS may be available for supported local payments.
- Discuss requirements, scope, feasibility, estimated cost, and schedule before accepting an order. Work begins after the order details and payment terms have been agreed upon.

### Refund policy

Commission payments are generally non-refundable. Please make sure the order details and scope are understood and agreed upon before work begins. If Yakka Works cannot complete an order that did not require a deposit, an appropriate resolution will be determined based on the circumstances.

### Copyright, credit, and licensing
