# Recycling Warehouse

**Versi 10 · Diperbarui 07-Sep-26**

## Deskripsi

Adds a functional Recycling Warehouse that promotes sustainability while generating monthly income. It features progressive income and capacity scaling that can be manually upgraded based on your city rank.
