# Nature Plus

**Version 1.3 · Updated 28-Oct-26**

## Description

Nature Plus, a plugin once known as Nature+ or Cytopia Nature Pack, has returned. It contains approximately 30 flora items, 21 ground items, 7 waters, 25 decals, 5 color decals, and 1 blueprint.

## Credits

Original graphics by Kingtut 101, Folesnaid, Yusuf8a684, RockFort, and Kekman (Valentine). Additional credits: Lobby Divinus, Just Anyone, and TheoTheoderich.
