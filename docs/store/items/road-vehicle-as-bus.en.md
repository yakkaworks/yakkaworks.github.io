# Road Vehicle as Bus

> Version 1 • 25 Nov 2026

Road Vehicle As Bus is a simple QoL plugin that lets you use regular road vehicles as buses in TheoTown. Assign routes to your vehicles and let them travel between designated stops, making it easier to create custom public transportation services and add more variety to your city's transit system.

## Installation guide

For installation steps, see the [TheoTown plugin installation guide](https://yakkaworks.github.io/library/theotown-tutorials/installing-plugins/).
