# TheoTown Plugin Commissions

**Commissions are open — starting at Rp35,000.** Have an idea for a TheoTown plugin but find sprite drawing, configuration, or debugging difficult? Yakka Works can help bring it to life.

Yakka Works has been creating TheoTown plugins since 2020. We are a Trusted Creator, hold a privilege code, and are part of the official TheoTown staff as a Discord Moderator.

For an initial estimate, use the [Commission Price Estimator](../../tools/commission-price-estimator/index.md). Final prices depend on requested features, complexity, and technical feasibility. Please discuss your requirements before ordering.

## Starter packages

### Basic building — Rp40,000 / US$5 (regional pricing)

- Maximum size of 4×4 tiles.
- Maximum 1 object.
- Manifest included.
- Night mode **or** rotation included; choosing both requires an additional fee.
- One in-game effect with a maximum radius of 16 tiles.
- Up to 3 revisions.

### Basic vehicle — Rp35,000 / US$4.25 (regional pricing)

For trains, buses, trucks, or cars.

- Maximum 2 objects.
- Night mode and rotation included.
- Manifest included.
- Functions as city transport.
- Up to 3 revisions.

## Request policy

### Requests we may accept

- NSFW, with some exceptions.
- Furry.
- Mecha.
- Other custom concepts, provided they are technically feasible.

### Requests we do not accept

- Skyscrapers, stadiums, or similar buildings.
- Overpowered (OP) plugin features.
- Excessive in-game income.
- Excessive effect radius.
- Excessive capacity.

Some NSFW kinks are not accepted. Every request is subject to technical feasibility and the creator's decision. Requests that do not meet this policy may be declined.

## Ordering and payment terms

- Orders below Rp60,000 do not require a deposit.
- Orders of Rp60,000 or more require a 25% deposit.
- For orders without a deposit, payment is due after the plugin is completed.
- Payment methods: QRIS, PayPal, Patreon, and Ko-fi.

## Refund policy

Commission purchases are **non-refundable**. Please make sure the order details are understood and agreed upon before work begins.

## Copyright, credit, and licensing

A minimum order of **Rp250,000 / US$50** is required to request special copyright terms, use without credit, or an exception to the standard license. Ownership and special licensing arrangements must be discussed and agreed upon before work begins.

The standard license stated for commission work is **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**. A custom license can be discussed for specific orders when rights to all included materials allow it. Any special terms must be agreed in writing before work begins.
